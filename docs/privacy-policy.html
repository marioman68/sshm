SSH Hub privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

SSH Hub ("the app") is made by Hardline Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • Your servers, SSH keys, settings and saved commands are stored only on your phone.
  • The app has no account and no cloud service. Hardline Labs does not receive your data.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show them (details below). SSH Hub Pro has no ads.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us:

  • The servers you add (names, addresses, user names, ports, groups, MAC addresses, notes, saved commands)
  • The SSH key the app can generate for you, and the host keys of servers you have trusted
  • Your settings

Passwords you type to log in to a server are kept in memory while the app is running and are never written to storage. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Network connections the app makes

  • Your servers. The app connects to the servers you add, over SSH, and checks whether they are reachable. Those connections go directly to your servers.
  • Network scan. When you start a scan, the app probes devices on your local network for SSH servers.
  • Built-in Tailscale (optional, Pro). If you turn it on and sign in, the app connects to Tailscale's coordination service so this device can join your tailnet. Tailscale receives what any Tailscale device sends: your Tailscale account login, a device name, the operating system, and network addresses. This is governed by Tailscale's privacy policy. SSH Hub turns off Tailscale's optional diagnostic log uploads.
  • Google Play. Purchases of SSH Hub Pro are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Advertising (free version)

The free version displays banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • the IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • information about the device and app (model, OS version, language)
  • interactions with ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn more in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Buying SSH Hub Pro removes the ads and the AdMob requests.

Analytics

The app does not use any analytics or tracking service of its own.

Children

SSH Hub is a tool for managing computer servers and is not directed at children under 13.
